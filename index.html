        How to read these pages

        Built means an instrument Dr. Jo developed or adapted; reused means an existing instrument she applied, with the original credited on its page. Every page opens with a record stating what the instrument measures, who it was studied with, and which parts of the page come from the article and which were written for this site. Sample items for reused instruments are illustrations, not the published items.

        These pages are study aids. They are not official instruments and are not endorsed by the authors. Go to the cited article for exact wording, scoring and evidence of validity before using anything here in research.
