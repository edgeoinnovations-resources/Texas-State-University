Score how well a geography lesson integrates geospatial technology

This is the TPACK-based rubric Huh & Jo (2023) used to evaluate 14 teachers' GST-integrated lessons — turned into a tool you can actually score with. Rate a lesson on the four criteria below and watch its TPACK profile build, compared against the study's teachers. It works as a diagnostic and as a design checklist while you build a lesson.

First, the idea it measures: TPACK

Good tech integration isn't tech skill bolted onto a lesson — it's the fusion of three knowledges. Hover a criterion below to see which overlap it lives in.

Technology · Content · Pedagogy

Overlap two circles and you get a blended knowledge; overlap all three and you reach TPACK — the ability to teach this content, with this pedagogy, using this tool. The rubric's four criteria each probe a different region:

1
Curriculum Goals & GST → probes TCK (does the tool serve the geography content/standards?)
2
Instructional Strategies & GST → probes TPK (does the tool serve the pedagogy?)
3
Technology Selection → probes TPACK (is this specific tool right for both?)
4
Fit → probes TPACK (do content, pedagogy & tool cohere as a whole?)

The rubric — score the lesson

Pick the level that best matches the lesson for each criterion. 4 = Outstanding · 3 = Accomplished · 2 = Developing · 1 = Beginning.
