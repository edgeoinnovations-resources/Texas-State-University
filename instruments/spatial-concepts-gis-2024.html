Why some concepts are easy and others aren't

The study borrows Glessmer & Brose's (2014) idea that a technical term's difficulty depends on how it collides with everyday language. Click a type to see which concepts fall in it.

Tip: the third type — hindering associations — is where the trouble lives.
The framework isn't quite enough. Two hard concepts don't fit cleanly. Interpolation is “neutral” (a brand-new word) yet students still struggled — some just gave up. And reference frame is hard not because of everyday baggage, but because instructors mostly use its synonym, coordinate system — so the term never sticks. The authors propose the framework needs more categories.
